### 🔍 Key Insights

- **Highest Profit Margin**: The Forever Love Bear (40%)
- **Top Revenue Channel**: gsearch (Google) – $800K
- **Landing Page A/B Test**: +6.56% uplift from `/home` to `/lander-1`
- **Billing Page A/B Test**: +37.3% uplift from `/billing` to `/billing-2`
- **Biggest Funnel Drop-off**: 38% from Product Detail → Cart
- **Free Channels**: Organic + Direct contributed $273K with zero ad spend
- **Repeat Customers**: 19% of orders, spending 15% more than new customers
- **Top Cross-sell Pair**: Mr. Fuzzy + Mini Bear – 3,126 times, $64K extra profit

---

### 💡 Strategic Recommendations

| Recommendation | Expected Impact |
|----------------|-----------------|
| **1. Fix the Funnel** – A/B test "Add to Cart" button design, color, and urgency messages | +10% conversion → $108K monthly revenue |
| **2. Reallocate Marketing Budget** – Shift 20% of Mr. Fuzzy budget to Love Bear (higher margin) | +$70K profit without increasing budget |
| **3. Launch Loyalty Program** – 10% discount on next order, early access to new products | +10% repeat customers → $372K annual revenue |
